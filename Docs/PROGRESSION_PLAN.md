## 2. The loadout reworked

**The problem you named:** take your limit, drop it on the floor, take the limit again, pick the dropped ones up — the per-person limit
means nothing. And the locker is a screen, not a place.

**Proposal:**
- **No per-person limits.** What limits you is the **armoury's stock** (the ship's real count) and **your belt**. Taking more just
  leaves less for the others and costs more to restock — a real choice, not a rule to get round.
- **Anything of the armoury's put down aboard the ship goes back into the armoury** when you leave the room it is in, or at once in the
  armoury itself — so nothing is lost or doubled lying on the ship's floor.
- **The armoury as a place** (with the ship rework): racks you take things off by hand, each with its count on a label, the mission's
  kit lit; the screen stays as the quick way (and for picking a whole kit at once).
- **Kit presets:** save a kit ("survey", "data job") and draw it in one press, as far as stock allows.
- **Mission kit** (copy unit, beacon parts, markers…) stays loaded in the shuttle by the contract, as now.

**Q2.** No per-person limits, things put down aboard go back in? *(recommended)* — or keep limits and count what each person has taken
in total since landing?
**Q3.** The physical armoury with the ship rework (screen until then)? *(recommended)*

## 3. Difficulty in words, not numbers

Now: "MODERATE (2/5)" and its reasons. **Proposal:** no number anywhere. The area's panel says what is known, as a crew would put it:
- **What lives there:** unknown (no sensors) · "signs of something, scattered" · "plain signs of a pack" · "a large animal's ground"
  (better sensors say more, never everything).
- **The ground:** "no air — suits sealed", "storm season", "dark there now", "far from any help".
- **How far out:** "close to home" · "on the frontier" · "deep out".
- One overall word the colour of its danger — *routine, hazardous, dangerous, lethal* — with no number.

*Done in part (FIX 754): the number is gone -- the word (ROUTINE, GUARDED, HAZARDOUS, DANGEROUS, LETHAL) and the reasons stay.*

**Q4.** Rewrite the reasons as the lines above (what lives there by sensor tier, the ground, how far out)? *(recommended)* — or drop the overall word too?
